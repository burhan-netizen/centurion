FAQs

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Talk to an advisor. The first conversation is free.

How do I know which loan is right for me?

You don't need to know. Tell us what you are planning, and we will recommend the solution and the bank that suit your goals best.

Is there any charge for the consultation?

No. Your first consultation with our team is completely free.

Will exploring options affect my credit score?

We review your profile first and approach only the lenders that fit, so you avoid unnecessary applications on your credit record.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the type of funding and the bank. Your dedicated advisor keeps you updated at every step until the funds are released.

I am self-employed. Can you help me?

Yes. We work with business owners, professionals and salaried individuals, and find options that match your income and profile.

Which cities do you serve?

We work with clients across Gujarat and all over India. Our team is headquartered in Ahmedabad, and we handle every case end to end, wherever you are.

How do partners get paid?

Partners earn a rewarding share on every successful case, with a clear structure shared upfront and payments made on time.
