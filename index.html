About Centurion

Working capital, secured and unsecured loans, structured by experts.

Centurion Strategic Ventures is a loan advisory and debt syndication firm based in Ahmedabad. We help MSMEs, businesses, professionals and salaried individuals raise the right funding through our network of 100+ banks and NBFCs, across all major cities in Gujarat and Pan India.

Led by directors with 20+ years of Banking, NBFC and financial expertise, we assess every case the way a lender would, then structure and place it where it secures the best terms, fastest decision and smoothest disbursement.

01

Working Capital

Cash credit, overdraft (OD/CC) and working capital limits structured around your turnover and business cycle.

02

Secured Loans

Loan against property, home loans, project finance and stressed asset resolution, at competitive rates.

03

Unsecured Loans

Business, professional and salaried personal loans, without collateral and with quick turnaround.

How It Works

A structured process, from assessment to disbursement.

Every case follows a disciplined, lender-ready process built for faster decisions and better terms.

  1. 01

    Profile assessment

    We analyse your requirement, income, financials, banking and credit profile to define the right facility and ticket size.

  2. 02

    Lender selection

    We structure the proposal and shortlist best-fit lenders from 100+ banks and NBFCs on appetite and pricing.

  3. 03

    Documentation & login

    We prepare a complete, lender-ready file, log in your case and coordinate credit, valuation and legal checks.

  4. 04

    Sanction & disbursal

    We negotiate terms, review the sanction letter and manage the process until funds are released.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about working with Centurion, as a client or as a partner.

For clients

Which loan is right for my requirement?

That is exactly where we come in. Our experts assess your profile, cash flows and objectives, then recommend the facility and lender best suited to you.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation with a Centurion expert is completely free and carries no obligation.

Will exploring options affect my credit score?

We evaluate your profile first and approach only the lenders most likely to approve, so your credit record stays protected from unnecessary enquiries.

How quickly can funds be disbursed?

Timelines depend on the facility and the lender. With complete documentation and the right lender match, we move your case forward at the fastest possible pace and keep you updated at every stage.

Do you work with MSMEs and self-employed professionals?

Absolutely. We work with MSMEs, traders, manufacturers, professionals and salaried individuals, and structure every facility around how you actually earn.

Which cities do you serve?

We serve clients across all major cities in Gujarat and Pan India, managing every case end to end, wherever you are.

For partners

Who can become a Centurion partner?

DSAs, loan connectors, Chartered Accountants, real estate professionals, insurance agents and mutual fund agents. Anyone with a client network that needs funding can partner with us.

What is the eligibility to become a partner?

A genuine client network and a commitment to ethical business. Share your details, complete a simple KYC and partner agreement, and you are ready to refer.

How much can partners earn?

Partners earn industry-leading payouts on every successfully disbursed case. The payout structure is shared transparently at onboarding and rewards both product and volume.

Which products can I refer?

Every Centurion facility, including working capital, OD/CC limits, loan against property, home loans, project finance, and business, professional and salaried personal loans.

When are partner payouts made?

Payouts are processed promptly after disbursement, with complete transparency on every case you refer.

How do I track the cases I refer?

Your dedicated relationship manager keeps you updated in real time, from login to sanction to disbursement.
